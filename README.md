## Overview

There is no single "best" Ramadan Umrah package. The right choice depends on:

- Total budget
- Hotel distance from the Haram
- Travel window: first 20 days or last 10 days of Ramadan
- Who is travelling: parents, children, friends or solo

## Economy vs Luxury

The visa and flight are usually similar across tiers. The real differences are:

1. **Distance to the Haram:** shuttle ride vs a 5-minute walk
2. **Room sharing:** 4–5 people vs 2 people

After a day of fasting and Taraweeh prayers, these two factors have a big
effect on comfort and energy.

## Choosing by Traveller Type

| Traveller | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| With elderly parents | Standard or Luxury. Prioritise hotel distance and confirm wheelchair assistance in advance. |
| First Umrah | Standard, travelling early in Ramadan when crowds are lighter. |
| Tight budget | Economy, but insist on a confirmed visa and a named hotel. |
| Last 10 nights | Any tier, booked 3–4 months ahead. Near-Haram rooms sell out first. |
| Couple or small group (2–10) | Ask for a block quote. Group booking can lower per-person cost. |

## What a 15-Day Package Should Include

- [x] Umrah visa processing and tracking
- [x] Return flights from Kolkata
- [x] Named hotels in Makkah and Madinah
- [x] Private transfers, including Makkah to Madinah
- [x] Group coordinator or guide
- [x] Meals as per tier

Typical split: **8–9 days in Makkah** and **6–7 days in Madinah**.

You can also check current entry requirements on Saudi Arabia's official
[Nusuk Umrah portal](https://umrah.nusuk.sa/).

## Why Hotel Distance Matters

"Walking distance" means nothing without a number.

| Hotel Distance | Typical Walk (busy prayer time) |
|----------------|---------------------------------|
| ~300 m | 4–5 minutes |
| ~1.5 km | 20–25 minutes |

Over five daily prayers for two weeks, the difference adds up to hours.
**Always ask for the hotel name and its distance in metres, in writing.**

## FAQ

**Which package is best for Ramadan?**
For most first-time families, Standard in the first half of Ramadan balances
cost and comfort.

**How much more does Ramadan cost?**
Often 20–40% more than other months, due to demand for flights and
near-Haram hotels.

**First 20 days or last 10 days?**
The first 20 are calmer and cheaper. The last 10 are spiritually significant
but more crowded and expensive.

**Can I customise the Makkah/Madinah split?**
Usually yes. Tell your agency your priority at booking.
